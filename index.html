About Cloudy

The girl behind the lens & the mic

"I'm an original and that's perfection in itself — you can't dull my sparkle. ✨"

— Cloudy Tone, July 2022

Cloudy Tone is a Mirzapur-based content creator, runway model and event anchor who has been creating memories since 2021 — blending high-fashion polish with the warmth of a hometown storyteller.

From walking the Lucknow India International Couture Week to hosting local launches and shooting festive drops for Mirzapur's boutiques and salons, she moves effortlessly between the ramp, the reel and the stage.

Her content lives where elegance meets everyday life — ethnic fashion, soft-glam beauty, festive nights and honest, no-filter moments — told in a voice her city recognises and trusts.
